A room for conversation, nothing more. There's no mission here, no task board, no workspace to tend — just an open thread with whichever gezel you've brought along.

Talk about whatever you like: think out loud, draft and redraft a piece of writing together, ask questions, or just keep company. The gezel replies in plain prose and stays in the conversation rather than going off to "do work."

This suits a warm companion gezel, and it's the natural home for a small on-device model with a tight context window — the kind that can't run tools or hold a big project brief, but is perfectly good at talking and writing.
