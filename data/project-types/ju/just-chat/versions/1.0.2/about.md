# Just chat

A room for conversation: ask questions, explore ideas, brainstorm, or draft and revise a piece of writing together. Your Conversationalist stays in the thread and answers in prose, with as much detail as the topic needs.

Research belongs in the conversation too. The gezel can look up information, read sources, and ask an adviser for another perspective, then bring the findings back to you.

There is no standing mission or task board to manage. If you ask for a concrete deliverable or a larger piece of work, the gezel can launch a suitable craftbook. Everyday questions do not need to become projects.
