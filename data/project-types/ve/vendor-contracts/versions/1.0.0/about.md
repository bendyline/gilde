The vendor and contract desk for {{business}}: every supplier and service the
business pays, what each contract costs a year, when its term ends, how much
notice it needs, and whether it renews on its own.

The **Output** tab shows the decisions coming up in the next 90 days, the
contracts that will renew automatically unless someone gives notice, where the
money goes by kind, every vendor, and the signed contracts on file. Drop a
contract into it to keep the paper beside the record.

Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved straight away: tell the office
administrator about a new contract, a price change, a renewal or a notice you
sent, and it goes on the desk before the reply. The desk records what the
business agreed and the dates it set; it is not legal advice. Where a clause or
a notice rule is unclear, the office administrator says so and suggests asking
the vendor in writing or a lawyer.
