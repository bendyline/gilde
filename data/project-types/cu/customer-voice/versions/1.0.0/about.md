Customer Voice for {{business}}: every review and survey answer, from
{{sources}}, sorted into themes with counts, so the business can see what
customers keep saying and whether it is getting better.

The **Output** tab shows each theme with this month's count against last
month's, the reviews that still need a reply, the actions agreed to fix
things, and every piece of feedback. Import a CSV of reviews there; the rows
arrive unsorted until the researcher sorts them.

Paste a review or a survey answer and the researcher logs it straight away,
exactly as the customer wrote it. Ask for one thing and you get that one,
saved straight away: a sorting pass, a reply draft or an action. Questions
about what customers say are answered in chat, from the board. Replies are
drafts until you post them, and they never promise a refund, discount or
change the business has not agreed. Feedback stays in this project.
