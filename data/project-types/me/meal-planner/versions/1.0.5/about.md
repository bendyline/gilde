The weekly answer to "what's for dinner?" — planned once, for {{household}}.
Your meal planner builds the week's menu from what's in the pantry, learns
what the table actually likes, and keeps the grocery run short.

The **Output** tab shows the week at a glance: every dinner, tonight
highlighted, and the grocery gap.

Ask for one dinner and you get that one, saved to the board straight away;
the meal planner offers the rest of the week after, never before. A pantry
change ("we're out of limes") goes on the board the moment it's mentioned.
