The sales pipeline for {{business}}: every deal from first contact to close,
with its value, its stage, the next follow-up and a weighted forecast.

The **Output** tab shows the follow-ups due, every open deal by stage, the
forecast by expected close month, and what was won and lost lately.

Tell the account manager about a new lead, a call, a proposal sent or a deal
won or lost, and it goes on the pipeline straight away. Ask for one thing and
you get that one, saved straight away. The forecast is an estimate from your
own numbers, not a promise, and contact details stay in this project.
