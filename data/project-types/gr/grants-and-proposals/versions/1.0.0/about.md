The grants and proposals desk for {{organization}}: every funding
opportunity and request for proposals worth a look, with its deadline, how
well it fits {{focus}}, what it asks for, and what came of it.

The **Output** tab shows the open opportunities by deadline with each
submission's requirements checklist, the full pipeline, and the outcomes so
far. The project's one reminder sits two weeks ahead of the next deadline.

Tell the fundraiser about a new opportunity, a finished requirement, a
deadline that moved or a decision that came in. Ask for one thing and you get
that one, saved straight away. The desk tracks what you apply for; it is not
legal, tax or financial advice. Eligibility and reporting rules are the
funder's, so the program officer is the one to ask, and your accountant for
anything about the books.
