Onboarding for {{business}}: one plan per new hire, from the welcome note
before their first day to the 90-day review.

The **Output** tab shows the steps due this week, overdue first, and each new
hire's plan by phase with who owns every step. Tick steps off right on the page.

Tell the training coordinator when someone is hired, when a step is done, or
what to add to a plan. Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved straight
away. The office administrator looks after accounts, equipment and paperwork;
the training coordinator looks after the first week, training and check-ins.

Hiring happens in Hiring Desk; certificates and refreshers live in Training
Tracker. Pay, tax and employment forms follow the rules where the business
operates; for those, ask an accountant or your local labor agency.
