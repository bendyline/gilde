long-form piece from idea to published, and everything it spins off after.

The **Output** tab shows the pipeline from idea to brief, draft, edit,
design and published; the editorial calendar; who is writing what; and the
newsletter, social posts and video chapters each piece is repurposed into.

Working agreement: ask for one thing and you get that one thing, saved
straight away. Pitch a piece, move one along or ask for a piece to be
repurposed, and the managing editor puts it on the board before describing
it. Facts, figures and quotes in the writing come from sources the business
can name; nothing is invented to fill a gap.
