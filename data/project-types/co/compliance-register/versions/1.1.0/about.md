The compliance register for {{business}}: every license, permit, filing,
insurance policy, certificate and inspection, with an owner, a due date and
the proof it was done.

The **Output** tab shows what is overdue, what is due in the next 60 days, the
whole register by category, and the evidence on file. Drop certificates and
receipts into it to keep the proof beside the obligation.

Tell the compliance officer about a renewal, a new permit or a finished
inspection and it goes on the register straight away. The register records
what the business has done; it is not legal advice, and where a rule is
unclear the officer names who to ask.
