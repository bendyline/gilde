The operations manual for {{business}}: how each job gets done, written down
step by step, filed by area, with an owner and the date someone last checked it.

The **Output** tab answers "how do I…" from the library itself, shows which
procedures are flagged or past their review date, and lists every procedure by
area. Each procedure is a plain markdown file under sops/, so anyone can open,
print or edit it.

Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved straight away: tell the librarian
how a job is done, that a procedure was checked, or that something changed, and
it goes in the library before the reply. Answers come from the written steps,
quoted as written. When the library has no procedure for something, the
librarian says so and offers to write one down with the person who does the
job. For food safety, health and safety or legal rules, the authority's own
guidance comes first; a procedure records how this business follows it.
