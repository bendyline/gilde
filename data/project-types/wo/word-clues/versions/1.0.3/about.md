A word-association game on the **Output** tab. The board holds sixteen words: six are
yours to find, nine are bystanders, and one is the trap.

Your clue-givers take turns. Each gives one word and a number, such as **OCEAN, 2**,
meaning two of your words go with "ocean". Tap the words you think they mean. You may
pick one more than the number. A bystander ends your turn, the trap ends the game, and
finding all six wins it.
