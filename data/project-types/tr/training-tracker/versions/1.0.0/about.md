The training tracker for {{business}}: who works here, which courses and
certificates each role needs, when each one was earned and when it runs out.

The **Output** tab shows what has lapsed or runs out in the next 30 days, a
matrix of people by courses, quizzes written from your own documents, and the
documents themselves. Drop checklists and guides into it and the training
coordinator can turn them into short quizzes.

Tell the training coordinator about a new starter, a finished course or a
certificate number. Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved straight
away. The tracker keeps the next certificate to run out on the project's
reminder, two weeks ahead.

It records what the business has done. Certificate rules come from the agency
or course provider that issues them; where a rule is unclear, ask them.
