Quotes and bids for {{business}}: each job priced line by line from
materials and labor at the business's own rates, the quotes sent and waiting,
the follow-ups, and what was won and lost.

The **Output** tab shows the follow-ups due, the quote builder with every line
and its exact total, all quotes with their status, the win and loss record,
and your rates, tax and terms.

Tell the estimator about a job and the quote is started straight away; give
it the hours and materials and it prices them. Ask for one thing and you get
that one, saved straight away. Totals are worked out from the lines and the
tax rate the business sets, to the cent. Which work is taxable is a question
for an accountant or the state revenue department, and code and permit
requirements belong to the licensed tradesperson and the local building
department; the estimator records what the business decides.
