The hiring desk for {{business}}: every open role with its requirements, the
candidates in each pipeline, the interviews coming up, and the scorecards your
interviewers fill in.

The **Output** tab shows this week's interviews, each role's pipeline from
sourced to hired, and every scorecard set against the role's requirements.

Tell the recruiter about a new role, a candidate, a booking or how an
interview went. Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved straight away.

How the crew works with you:

- The recruiter organizes, drafts and summarizes the evidence. You make every
  hiring decision: who moves forward, who gets an offer, who doesn't.
- Candidates are rated only on the role's job-related requirements, never on
  age, race, religion, sex, pregnancy, disability, national origin or any other
  protected trait.
- Candidate details stay in this project on this computer.
- Hiring rules differ by place. For pay, offers, background checks or what you
  may ask, check with an employment lawyer or your local labor agency.
