The support desk for {{business}}: every customer question as a ticket, from
new to resolved, with a suggested reply drawn from the business's own help
articles in `kb/`.

The **Output** tab shows what needs a reply (urgent first, then oldest), what
is waiting on the customer, every ticket by status and category, and the
questions no help article answers yet. Add the help articles you already have
to the knowledge base there.

Tell the support lead what a customer asked and it becomes a ticket straight
away. Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved straight away: a reply is
saved on its ticket, in a {{voice}} voice, as a draft for you to send. Tickets
come from you and the crew; a mail connector (Gmail, IMAP or Microsoft 365
mail) is optional and lets the support lead read the inbox too. A reply never
promises a refund, discount, date or exception the business has not stated.
