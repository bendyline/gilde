The goals board for {{team}}: each goal with its owner, its due date and the
key results that show whether it is moving, the weekly check-ins, and the
actions agreed in meetings.

The **Output** tab shows every active goal, furthest behind first, with each
key result's current value against its target, which goals have not had a
check-in this week, and the open actions with their owners and dates.

Tell the planner what moved ("we signed two more clients"), who checked in,
or what a meeting agreed. Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved
straight away. The board does the adding up, so progress is never worked out
by hand, and the goals and the calls on them stay the team's own.
