An **Email / Inbox** project connects a mail account and syncs its threads
into the project as immutable, per-thread markdown — one folder per
conversation, with frontmatter you can `grep`. From there a gezel can triage
what's new, draft replies, summarize long threads, and keep track of what
still needs a response.

Nothing is sent without your say-so: drafts land in an outbox and go out only
after you approve them, to recipients on an allow-list you control. Everything
stays on your disk.
