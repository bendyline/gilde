The shift roster for {{business}}: the week's shifts, who is working each one,
when people can't work, and the hours everyone is on.

The **Output** tab shows the week as a grid, the open spots with who is free to
take them, everyone's hours against their limits, availability, and the rules
you set.

Tell the shift scheduler about a new shift, a change in someone's availability
or a spot to fill, and it is recorded straight away. The roster checks
coverage, overlaps, rest and hours itself, against the rules you state and no
others. The scheduler proposes; you approve each week on the page. This is not
legal advice: for what labor law requires, ask your state or local labor office
or an employment lawyer.
