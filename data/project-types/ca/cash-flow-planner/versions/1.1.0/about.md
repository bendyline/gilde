The cash plan for {{business}}: what is in the bank, the invoices and bills
expected over the next 13 weeks, and the week cash would dip below the cushion
the business wants to keep, in {{currency}}.

The **Output** tab shows the week-by-week forecast, the warning week, the plan
itself, and each what-if beside it. A what-if (a client paying late, a new
hire, a lost contract) is tried on its own and never changes the plan.

Working agreement: mention a bank balance, an invoice, a bill or a payment that
came in and it goes into the plan straight away. Ask for one thing and you get
that one thing, saved before it is described. Ask "what if" and the bookkeeper
tries it as a scenario, leaving the plan as it was.

A forecast is arithmetic on what you expect, not financial advice. For
borrowing, credit lines or tax payments, talk to your accountant or banker.
