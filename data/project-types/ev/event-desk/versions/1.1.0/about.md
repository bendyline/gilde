The event desk for {{event}}: the venue shortlist and the one you book, every
vendor with its contract and deposits, the invite list with RSVPs and dietary
needs, the run of show, and the budget against what is signed.

The **Output** tab compares the venues for your headcount, shows which deposits
are due, who is coming and what they can eat, and how much of the budget is
still uncommitted.

Tell the event planner about a quote, an RSVP or a payment and it goes on the
desk straight away. Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved before it is
described. Money is kept to the cent and dates as you give them. The desk
tracks what you agreed and paid; it does not interpret contract terms, so for
cancellation or liability clauses ask the vendor or a lawyer. Guest details and
dietary needs stay in this project.
