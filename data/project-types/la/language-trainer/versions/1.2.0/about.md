A project for learning **{{language}}** by actually speaking it. Pick a scene on the
**Output** tab (ordering at a café, lost luggage, a pharmacy visit), and your tutor plays
the other person while you practice in {{language}}.

Every mistake you make becomes a correction under the tutor's reply and a card in your
review deck. The deck brings each correction back a day later, then after three days,
then after a week, until it sticks. The Output tab shows your level, your day streak,
the reviews due, and the scenes you can play.
