Client delivery for {{studio}}: every client project with its milestones,
the approvals waiting on clients, and the status each client gets every week.

The **Output** tab shows what is out with a client and for how long, what is
late on our side, each project's milestones in order, and a status draft for
every active project.

Tell the planner that a milestone started, went to the client, came back with
changes or was approved, or that a date slipped, and the board changes straight
away. Ask for one thing and you get that one, saved before it is described.
Dates move by the days you say; the board works out the new date. Billing lives
in Freelance Office. Proposals and statements of work drafted here are for you
to review; for contract terms, ask your lawyer.
