The stockroom for {{business}}: every item kept on the shelf, how many are
left, the point at which to reorder it, and who supplies it.

The **Output** tab shows what is out or running low, the purchase orders on the
way and waiting for you, the whole stock list, and each supplier with their
lead time.

Tell the stock keeper what came in, what got used or what you counted, and it
is saved straight away; the stock list does the arithmetic. Ask for this week's
orders and you get one draft per supplier, filled back up to your par levels.
Drafts are yours to check and place: nothing is ordered until you say it was
sent, and prices are the ones you gave.
