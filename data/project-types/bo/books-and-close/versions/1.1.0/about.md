The books for {{business}}: every receipt, sale and bill in one categorized
ledger, kept in {{currency}} and closed month by month on a checklist.

The **Output** tab shows the receipts inbox, the entries that still need a
category, the month-close checklist, a P&L snapshot for the month being closed
and this month so far, and the ledger itself. Drop receipts into the inbox and
the bookkeeper books them.

Working agreement: mention a payment, a sale or a receipt and it goes into the
ledger straight away, with its receipt beside it. Ask for one thing and you get
that one thing, saved before it is described. A closed month stays closed until
you reopen it.

These are the books as recorded, not tax or legal advice. For what is
deductible, what you owe or how to file, ask your accountant or a tax
professional.
